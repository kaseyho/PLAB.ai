How it works
Python rules
Check landing times, gates, routes, fuel and crews. Failed or unknown checks block a plan.
Three LLM agents
ATC, Pilot and Ground each send one message per turn. They must agree on the same plan within three turns. Otherwise, fixed rules choose a checked backup.
You
Approve or reject with a reason. Only approval books resources in the simulator, after a final check.

Who this helps, and why
One decision
An airport duty manager needs one landing time, taxi route and gate for a delayed flight.
Today
ATC, pilots and ground staff use phone calls and manual updates. When one plan changes, everyone must recheck.
Cost and risk
Extra waiting uses fuel and can add crew overtime. We show simulated trade-offs; real time and money savings are unmeasured.
Prototype boundary
One arrival with simulated resources. Real airport authorities would retain their own approval powers.
