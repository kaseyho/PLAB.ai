PLAB Arrival disruption desk
Simulation

Re-plan a disrupted arrival

When a flight is delayed, three desks each look after their own part: air traffic control (runway and spacing), the pilot (fuel and crew hours) and ground operations (gate and handling crew). Here they negotiate a new landing time and gate. Every option is checked against fixed safety rules first. Nothing changes until you approve it.
