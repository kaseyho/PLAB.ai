# Arrival desk artwork

`airport-model.png` is an original illustrative airport miniature generated with
the built-in image generation tool on 31 October 2026. It is decorative artwork,
not an operational map or a view of the current scenario.

The visual direction takes inspiration from Congrue's restrained architecture,
large type and technical product imagery. No Congrue assets or copy are reused.
Public Sans (body text), Manrope (headings) and IBM Plex Mono (metadata) are
self-hosted under their included SIL Open Font Licenses in `../fonts/`.
Public Sans is sourced from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/publicsans).
The brand and role marks are native SVGs.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original hero illustration for PLAB.ai, an airport arrival coordination prototype. Create a refined high-end isometric architectural scale model of a small airport on a low rectangular dark graphite plinth: a precise miniature runway with fine white markings, parallel taxiway with subtle pale mint guidance lights, a low contemporary glass-and-concrete terminal with two jet bridges, a slender control tower, one white passenger jet approaching the runway and one small parked jet at a gate. Three-quarter orthographic view from above; entire model visible, centered, wide landscape 3:2 composition. Restrained architectural product-render aesthetic, immaculate fine details and realistic materials, matte charcoal pavement, warm ivory buildings and aircraft, subtle pale teal windows and lights. Soft directional studio lighting from upper left. Isolated on a uniform solid dark warm graphite background #242826 matching the website. No skyline, no surrounding city, no people, no big labels, no typography, no logo, no watermark, no neon glow, no gradients or decorative particles. This is an illustrative airport miniature, not a real operational map. The miniature should fill most of the canvas with generous clean dark margins, elevated and visually striking.
